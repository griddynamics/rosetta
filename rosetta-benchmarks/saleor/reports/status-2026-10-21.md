**Takeaways so far**
- Grill's interview caught the hidden requirements up front (length limit, translation, webhooks/CSV export). Baseline missed exactly those and needed 3 follow-ups; ~40 % of its cost went into fixing them.
- Baseline is the cheapest; Grill costs more up front but almost nothing after; Superpowers is the most expensive so far.
- Diff judge (added 13 Nov): Superpowers 73.5 > Grill 71.6 > Baseline 66.2; none of the three is advised merge-ready (one or two majors each).

**Caveats:** one run per method (n = 1), operator scores come from different operators; the diff judge is advice (it sees the method names), small setup differences between runs (each report, section 8).
