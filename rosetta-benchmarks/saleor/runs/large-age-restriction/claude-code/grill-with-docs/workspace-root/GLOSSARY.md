# Glossary

**Customer**:
A person with an account who shops on the storefront. Guests are visitors without an account and are not Customers.

**Birthday**:
A Customer's date of birth. Personal data, set by the Customer and visible only to them and a super-admin.

**Age-restricted product**:
A product that can only be sold to Customers who meet the Minimum age. Products are unrestricted unless marked.

**Minimum age**:
The least age, in completed years, a Customer must have to buy Age-restricted products. One value for the whole store (18), not a property of an individual product.

**Eligible**:
A Customer is eligible on the day they reach the Minimum age, judged by date in the shop's time zone. A 29 February birthday counts as 1 March in non-leap years.
